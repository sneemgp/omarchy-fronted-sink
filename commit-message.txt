fix(audio): resolve the fronted sink from the running graph, not only from a shipped tuning

`omarchy audio tuning fronted-sink` answers from the matched tuning's
sink_pattern, through tuned_hardware_sink. A tuning installed by hand -- one with
no directory under default/audio/tunings -- has no pattern to read, so the command
printed nothing and exited 1 while a tuning was installed and fronting.

Both consumers of that answer then stopped hiding the physical sink:
omarchy-audio-sink-availability reported it available, and
omarchy-audio-output-switch kept it as a rotation stop, so picking it bypasses the
tuning. That contradicts docs/audio-tuning.md, which states the fronted sink is
hidden, without qualifying the promise by where the tuning came from.

When no shipped tuning answers, resolve the physical sink from the installed
tuning's own output stream instead -- the resolution this script already has as
tuning_downstream_sink, which follows the filter-chain's stream down to the sink
underneath. The declaration stays the first answer, because it is the most
precise one when it exists; the graph is the fallback, not the replacement.

The guard matters: while the chain is idle or unlinked,
omarchy-audio-output-sink falls back to printing the sink it was given, and
reporting the tuning sink as the sink to hide would be worse than the bug.

The same resolution fixes a second consequence in `off`: removing a hand-installed
tuning previously left the default sink pointing at a sink that no longer existed,
because there was no physical sink to restore it to.
